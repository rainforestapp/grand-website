How it works

Grace is always on the other end.

Grace works just like a phone conversation. Pick up when you want to talk. Have Grace ring with a reminder or a family message. Hang up when you’re done.

Everyday help

A patient voice.

Ask Grace about the weather, the calendar, a recipe, a name, a letter, or whatever comes up. The point is simple: no screen, no app, no waiting.

Proactive support

Useful nudges.

Grace can ring for medication, appointments, family messages, and check-ins. Those moments can be turned on, adjusted, or turned off, so the phone stays helpful rather than nagging.

A normal phone

Still a phone.

Make and receive ordinary calls with family, friends, carers, and anyone else. Grace should feel like the phone she already knows, not another device to manage.

What Grace helps with

Everyday support, made simple.

Grace is for the small, ordinary moments that make a day feel easier. The things that are too minor for a care plan, but still matter.

Reminders

Pills, appointments, routines.

Medication, birthdays, bins, appointments, and the small things that are easy to forget.

Questions

Quick answers by voice.

Weather, recipes, calendars, letters, instructions, names, dates, and anything else that comes up.

Family messages

Notes read aloud.

Children, grandchildren, and close friends can send messages for Grace to read over the phone, with spoken replies.

Conversation

A friendly voice between calls.

Some days are quieter than others. Grace is there for a question, a story, a joke, a memory, or an unhurried chat.

For the older adult

Useful without feeling like a care product.

Grace helps her keep track of the day, remember what matters, ask questions, hear from family, and call people without needing to find a smartphone or learn a new app.

She stays in control.

Grace is something she chooses to use. Pick it up, answer it, ignore it, or hang up.

She gets help without fuss.

A small question does not have to become a text chain, a search result, or a call she worries is interrupting someone.

She keeps real relationships close.

Grace can help place calls, pass on messages, and make it easier to reach the people she already loves.

For family

Feel closer, even when life gets busy.

Grace helps family stay involved in the ordinary moments that are easy to miss: a morning reminder, a quick message, a check-in after lunch, or a nudge to call.

Send the little things.

A note from a grandchild. A reminder about tomorrow. A message that says, “thinking of you,” read aloud when it matters.

Share the load.

Siblings and close family can coordinate reminders and messages, so support is not trapped in one person’s head.

Make calls more meaningful.

Grace can help surface the right nudge at the right time, so family calls become connection rather than constant checking.

Grace doesn’t replace family.

She helps family show up more often.

Peace of mind

Gentle reassurance, not hovering.

With permission, Grace can help family notice changes: missed reminders, unanswered check-ins, fewer conversations, or optional home sensors that suggest someone may need a little extra support.

The goal is not to watch over someone. It is to notice when care might be needed, while preserving independence and dignity.

Clear boundaries

What Grace isn’t.

  • Not a camera. Grace is built around voice and consent, not watching.
  • Not secretly listening. Grace is designed around intentional phone conversations and proactive calls that can be enabled or turned off.
  • Not a medical device. Grace does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional care.
  • Not a replacement for family. Grace supports daily life and strengthens connection, but people still matter most.
  • Not something to hide. Grace only works if the older adult understands it, wants it, and feels respected by it.

FAQ

Questions families ask first.

Is Grace a normal phone?

Yes. Grace is designed to work as a regular phone, with helpful voice features built in.

Does the older adult need a smartphone?

No. Grace is made for people who prefer to pick up a phone and talk.

Can Grace ring proactively?

Yes. Grace can ring for reminders, family messages, and check-ins when those features are enabled.

Can family use Grace too?

Yes. Family members can send messages, reminders, and check-ins, and receive gentle updates where enabled.

Is Grace always listening?

Grace is designed around intentional use, like a phone. Pick it up when you want to talk, and hang up when you are done.

Can Grace replace carers or family calls?

No. Grace is there to support daily life and strengthen connection, not replace people.
