Privacy Policy

Last updated September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Grand (“Grand,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website, join our waitlist, or participate in our private alpha using the Grand app, hub, and sensors (the “Service”). The private alpha is invitation-only and available only to adults and households located in the United States. It is not a medical or emergency service.

Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you give us. When you join the waitlist we collect your contact details — a phone number or an email address, depending on the sign‑up form. If you choose to answer our optional follow‑up questions, we also collect your full name, ZIP code, your reason for being interested in Grand, and whether the person Grand is for lives alone.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, approximate location derived from it, browser and device type, referring page, and how you interact with our site.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Some of our advertising partners use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to measure advertising. Our PostHog website analytics is configured in cookieless mode. See the sections below for more detail.
  • Caregiver and account information. For alpha participants, we collect information such as name, phone number, account and authentication records, caregiver relationship, settings, support communications, and acceptance of the Grand Alpha Terms.
  • Household and Loved One information. Caregivers or Grand staff may provide a Loved One’s name or nickname, phone number, home address, time zone, household membership, and information needed to install and operate the Service.
  • Sensor information and audio recordings. The hub and sensors collect information about activity in the home, including motion and ambient audio. During the alpha, raw audio recordings and other sensor data are sent to Grand’s servers. Audio may incidentally include voices or other sounds in the home. Grand does not use cameras or collect video or biometric identifiers through the Service.
  • Activity estimates and alerts. We generate estimates and inferences about presence, rooms, household activities, routines, changes, and events that may need a caregiver’s attention. We also keep associated alerts and caregiver actions.
  • App and device information. We collect app usage, device and push-notification identifiers, IP address, diagnostic logs, crash and error information, and coarse interaction events used to operate, secure, understand, and improve the Service.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Add you to the waitlist and notify you as Grand nears launch;
  • Understand who Grand is for and decide where to launch first;
  • Set up and manage caregiver accounts and households;
  • Process sensor data, estimate household activities and routines, and provide information and automated alerts to caregivers;
  • Operate, test, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the Service, including its activity-detection models. Limited authorized Grand staff may review sensor data and audio recordings for debugging and accuracy improvement;
  • Provide support and communicate with alpha participants;
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website and app;
  • Measure and improve our marketing, including understanding which advertisements led people to sign up; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Website analytics

We use PostHog to understand aggregate website usage, including pages visited, referral and campaign sources, device and browser information, calls-to-action clicked, and completion of the waitlist and onboarding steps. PostHog is configured in cookieless mode for anonymous visits and does not set its own analytics cookies or persistent browser identifiers. If you join the waitlist, we generate a random candidate identifier, keep it for the duration of that browser tab’s session, and share it with PostHog so we can connect the website journey to the corresponding waitlist entry. We do not send PostHog the phone number, email address, name, ZIP code, or free-text answers you enter into our forms. This analytics data is hosted in PostHog’s United States region.

Product analytics and diagnostics

We use PostHog for limited product analytics, such as app sessions, onboarding progress, screens and features used, alert actions, notification actions, settings, and coarse error events. We also use Sentry for crash and error monitoring. We do not intentionally send raw audio or sensor events to these analytics and diagnostics tools.

Advertising and conversion tracking

We advertise Grand on third‑party platforms, and we use their advertising tools — including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and the Reddit Pixel — to measure how well those advertisements work.

When you sign up for the waitlist, we treat that sign‑up as a conversion event and share it with our advertising partners. In practice this means that the fact that a click on an ad (or a visit to our site) resulted in a waitlist sign‑up is passed back to platforms such as Meta and Reddit, along with technical identifiers those platforms use to attribute the conversion. This lets us see which campaigns drive sign‑ups, avoid wasting spend, and reach more people who are likely to be interested in Grand.

These advertising partners may combine the information they receive with data they already hold about you, and may use it to build audiences and personalize the ads you see. Their use of your information is governed by their own privacy policies. You can learn about and control this tracking through Meta’s Privacy Policy and Reddit’s Privacy Policy, as well as through the cookie and ad‑personalization settings in your browser and on those platforms.

How we share information

We share information as follows:

  • With authorized caregivers. Caregivers in the same household can receive and view household information, activity estimates, alerts, and actions taken by other caregivers.
  • With service providers that help us provide hosting and storage, databases, analytics, error monitoring, communications, push notifications, support, and website and waitlist operations. These include Render, PostHog, Sentry, Apple, and other providers acting for Grand;
  • With advertising and analytics partners as described in “Advertising and conversion tracking” above;
  • For legal reasons, such as to comply with the law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety, and property of Grand and others; and
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, financing, or acquisition, in which case we will require the recipient to honor this Policy.

Where information is processed

Grand and its service providers process and store alpha information in the United States.

Your choices and rights

  • Email. You can ask us to stop emailing you, or to remove you from the waitlist, at any time — just contact us using the details below.
  • Cookies and ad tracking. You can block or delete cookies in your browser and opt out of interest‑based advertising through your platform and browser settings.
  • Access and deletion. Depending on where you live and subject to applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Alpha participants may also ask for an export of their information. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and authority to request information about a household or another person.
  • Stopping household monitoring. A Loved One may withdraw permission by telling a caregiver. The caregiver must then contact Grand and stop using and remove the equipment.

Data retention

We generally retain alpha product and sensor information, including raw audio recordings, for up to one year after collection. We retain waitlist information for as long as needed to contact potential testers and support our business. We may retain account, consent, legal, security, and backup records for longer where reasonably necessary. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

The Service and private alpha are for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly permit children to participate. If you believe we have collected a child’s personal information, please contact us.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be made clear on this page.

Contact us
